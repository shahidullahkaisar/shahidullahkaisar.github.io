Current research

MS thesis · ongoing

Nonlinear optical response of black tea grades studied by the Z-scan technique

Using a Gaussian-beam Z-scan setup, I measure the nonlinear refractive index (n₂) of different black tea grades (GBOP, DUST, CD). The work characterises self-focusing and self-defocusing behaviour, compares the strength of the nonlinearity across grades, and examines saturation at high intensity — relevant to optical power limiting with low-cost, naturally abundant materials.

Research interests

Computational materials science

Density functional theory for electronic structure, band structures, density of states and optical properties. Currently working with Quantum ESPRESSO, including molecular dynamics runs and analysis of temperature, energy, MSD and RDF.

Nonlinear optics of materials

How refractive index and absorption change under intense light, and how to measure those changes reliably — Z-scan, optical limiting, and characterisation of novel and natural materials.

Quantum computing for materials

Variational quantum algorithms and quantum simulation as future tools for electronic-structure problems that scale badly on classical hardware.

Machine learning in materials

Interested in data-driven property prediction and accelerating simulation workflows.
