about me

Roots & branches

I'm a physics major at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, and I've fallen in love with the question of why materials do what they do. In the lab, I measure how intense laser light bends inside black tea solutions (yes, really — it's my master's thesis!). On my laptop, I simulate electrons with density functional theory.

I want to become a computational materials scientist — someone who designs new materials inside a computer before anyone makes them in a lab. Quantum computing for materials is the frontier I'm most excited about.

When I'm not doing physics, I'm outside with a camera. Nature is the best-designed material system there is. 🌿

MY MOTIVATION

"Nature already solved it — I just want to read the solution."

Every leaf, crystal, and cup of tea is running physics we barely understand. Computation is how I get to peek at the source code.
